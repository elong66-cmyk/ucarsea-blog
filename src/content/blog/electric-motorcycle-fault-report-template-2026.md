Agreeing on a fault-report format before an order can make it easier for a dealer and a supplier to discuss the same unit. Ask the supplier which details its technical team needs and who receives the initial report.

### Give the supplier enough context

Record the model and serial reference, the symptom, when it occurred and any display message. Include a photo or video if it can be captured safely. Keep the customer’s private details outside the supplier copy unless they are necessary and the sharing is authorized. Ask what to do if the unit cannot be identified or the symptom cannot be reproduced.

### Agree who can test, advise and close the case

Identify the party responsible for initial advice and any authorized service work. Ask the supplier to point to the applicable service instructions. Avoid asking customers to open a battery or modify the electrical system to complete the report. Agree what evidence will be kept when the case is closed and handle parts, labour and transport under the order terms.

If after-sales support is part of your sourcing brief, tell the supplier what your local service team can handle and what requires its support.
