A listing headed “2023 model” may describe a vehicle first registered in a later year. That is not automatically a mistake, but the two dates answer different questions. For a Kazakhstan-bound sourcing enquiry, keep them separate before discussing a deposit or comparing an overseas listing with local stock.

## Ask for three dates, not one year

Record the China-market model-year designation, the actual manufacturing date and the first-registration date. If the manufacturing date has not been verified, write “not confirmed.” Neither a listing title nor its website upload date establishes the manufacturing date.

Match the actual vehicle identifier and documents privately during the order review. Do not publish an owner's identity or unredacted registration documents as marketing evidence. A supplier's statement is a lead to check, not an independent verification.

## Keep mileage evidence attached to the unit

Record the supplier's stated mileage and the date of the evidence. When a dashboard photograph and listing text differ, resolve the difference instead of selecting the lower figure for advertising. A photograph of an odometer does not, by itself, establish a complete mileage history or rule out repairs.

For each shortlist item, ask which facts are documented, which come from the supplier, and which remain unresolved. Keep exact trim, engine, transmission, steering configuration and disclosed repair information beside the price.

## Know what is available before and after the deposit

Before an agreed deposit, UCarsea provides available vehicle photographs and basic information. A full inspection report is not promised at this stage.

After the agreed deposit, vehicle inspection and the report are arranged under the written order terms. Before paying, agree the deposit amount, inspection scope, inspection costs, timing and refund conditions. Do not assume that every inspection is free or that every deposit is unconditionally refundable.

If inspection reveals a major vehicle-condition issue, the normal next step is to look for a suitable replacement. If an acceptable replacement cannot be agreed, a refund is arranged under the agreed terms. That workflow is not a substitute for written fee and refund provisions.

## Reconfirm stock without assuming a reservation

Stock and prices are dynamic. Request a dated availability check before the next purchase decision and again before payment. If a shortlist is about a week old, request a fresh check: the vehicle may already have been sold. A quote validity period does not, by itself, reserve a vehicle.

Keep the vehicle-only price separate from origin transport, export costs, freight and destination charges. Confirm the requested city and handover point. For Kazakhstan, vehicle eligibility, documentation, valuation and any registration or tax obligations need current, unit-specific checks with the relevant authorities and your local specialist. This article does not state a duty rate or guarantee admission or registration.
