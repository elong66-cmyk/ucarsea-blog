Put the selected vehicle reference, the quote currency and the quote basis at the top of each offer. Ask the supplier which costs are included and which still need a separate quotation. Keep the time and date of the quote beside the amount so your colleagues can tell which version they are using.

## Compare the same parts of each offer

Create a row for the selected vehicle, then separate rows for freight and destination charges. Record who provides each confirmed quote and who will confirm the remaining costs. If a price is a website reference for a specific vehicle, carry that label into the comparison sheet. Record the basis in the supplier’s own words before converting it into your working currency.

## Keep the unpriced items visible

If the route, destination or vehicle availability still needs checking, leave it visible in your comparison and give it an owner. Ask for an updated offer after any of those details change. Share the final version and its date with the colleagues who will book transport and receive the vehicle.

When requesting a sourcing review, send the vehicle type, quantity and destination and ask which items the next quote will cover.
