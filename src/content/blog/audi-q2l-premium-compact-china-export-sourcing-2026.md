## Why a "small" Audi is a bigger opportunity than it looks

Dealers chasing China-sourcing margin default to two plays: high-volume budget sedans, or big-ticket luxury SUVs (Q7, GLE, X5). The Q2L sits in a gap most outreach ignores — **compact footprint, full badge prestige, entry price point**.

In China, the Q2L is a volume-produced domestic-market model (FAW-Audi), which means:
- **Deep used supply** — unlike an imported Q2 in Europe, the China Q2L has a real secondary market with real comparables.
- **Lower entry cost than the badge implies** — buyers pay for the four rings, not the trim tier.
- **LHD by default** — China-market Audis are LHD, no conversion needed for export to Iraq, Gulf, West/East Africa.

The buyer psychology matters here: in markets where a used Camry and a used Q2L land at a similar retail price, the Audi badge wins the walk-in every time. Margin follows badge pull, not just spec sheet.
