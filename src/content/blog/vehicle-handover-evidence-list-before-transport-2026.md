Selecting a used vehicle and preparing it for handover are different stages. Before the vehicle moves to the next party, the working file should make clear which unit is involved, what has been checked and what remains unresolved.

This is an evidence-management checklist, not a substitute for an inspection, transport contract or destination-specific document advice.

## Match the unit to the file

Use an internal vehicle reference and the appropriate identity evidence to connect the quotation, photographs and documents to the same unit. Share sensitive identifiers only with the authorized parties and through an appropriate channel. Public marketing images need not expose private registration or ownership information.

Keep original source files where permitted. Note when photographs were obtained and who supplied them. A complete set of angles makes review easier, but the number of images does not establish mechanical condition, ownership, accident history or current availability.

## Record checks as checks, not assumptions

List the inspection or verification work actually performed, by whom and when. Separate a supplier statement from independent findings. An unresolved item should remain marked pending, not be converted to “passed” because the vehicle has already been advertised.

If mileage, configuration, visible condition or documents differ from the agreed information, stop and resolve the difference with the relevant parties before treating the original approval as still applicable.

## Confirm the receiving instructions

Identify the party authorized to receive the vehicle, the agreed handover point, necessary contact details and the transport instructions. Confirm the carrier’s acceptance requirements with the carrier, including any vehicle-specific limitations. Do not infer acceptance from a previous shipment involving another unit.

Document which keys, accessories and permitted documents should accompany the vehicle. The exact list depends on the transaction and route; avoid presenting a universal checklist as a legal requirement for every destination.

## Preserve the actual handover receipt

Preparation photos, a booking request and a transport receipt prove different things. Record the actual handover date and receiving confirmation when available. If there is no receipt, report that it has not been obtained rather than claiming the vehicle has shipped.
