Two adult electric motorcycles can have different riding layouts even when their photographs look similar. For a dealer model review, identify the exact model and variant before comparing measurements or asking for a demonstration.

## Build a model-specific record

Request the manufacturer's stated seat height, wheel sizes, vehicle dimensions and weight, with units and the relevant configuration. Ask whether the stated weight includes the battery and which components are standard or optional. Keep a dated source for each specification instead of merging figures from several similar models.

Clear side and cockpit photographs can show the relationship between the seat, handlebars, foot supports and controls. Ask which braking and drive controls are fitted to the offered variant. Do not infer their operation from a photograph alone.

## Separate measurements from suitability

Catalogue dimensions describe the product. They do not establish that a particular rider will be comfortable or able to control it. If a demonstration or fit assessment is needed, arrange an appropriate local evaluation and follow the manufacturer's instructions. Record what was actually assessed and what remains open.

## Bring the review back to the buying brief

Tell the supplier whether the enquiry concerns adult off-road recreation, a dealer display or another proposed use. Confirm the product configuration and relevant destination requirements separately. A riding-layout review does not establish road registration, import approval or permission to use a vehicle in a particular place.

For an initial [UCarsea Moto dealer enquiry](https://moto.ucarsea.com/dealers.html), provide the intended use, destination and model reference. Request the exact supporting documents before relying on a catalogue comparison.
