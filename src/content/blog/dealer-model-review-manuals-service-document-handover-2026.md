Before a dealer evaluates an electric motorcycle model, agree which documents are needed and who will supply them. Keep the pack tied to the exact model, variant and configuration. A folder of generic brand literature can leave important questions unanswered.

## Ask for usable operating information

Request the applicable user manual, control descriptions and charging instructions. Check the language, revision date and model reference. Where the supplied charger or battery differs between variants, ask which instructions belong to the proposed combination rather than assuming one manual covers every version.

## Define the service handover

List the service information available to the dealer, the intended support contact and the process for raising a technical question. Ask whether parts diagrams, maintenance information or training material exist for the exact variant. Record unavailable documents as gaps. Possession of a parts list does not prove that every part is stocked or immediately compatible with another model.

If warranty or repair responsibilities are being discussed, ask for the proposed written scope and the parties involved. Do not infer a warranty term or reimbursement arrangement from a sales brochure or an informal conversation.

## Keep shipment documents as a separate workstream

Identify which vehicle and battery documents the proposed shipment may require and ask the appropriate parties to review their applicability. A general manual is not a substitute for transaction-specific transport or destination checks.

The purpose of the pack is to support a model review and expose missing information before an order decision.
