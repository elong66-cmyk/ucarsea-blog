An electric motorcycle can fit a showroom floor and still require planning when it arrives packed. Ask how the exact supplied model will be presented at receipt and request the manufacturer's instructions before assigning space or staff.

### Check what arrives and what needs preparation

Confirm the supplied configuration and ask which parts, accessories and printed materials are packed with it. Request the external package dimensions and unpacking instructions for that configuration. Ask what tools, checks or qualified service work the manufacturer requires before the first demonstration. Have your own team determine whether its space and facilities meet those instructions.

### Record the demo setup your team can support

Identify who will receive, prepare and document the unit. Before a demo, compare the actual pack contents with the agreed list. Set aside anything you cannot identify and obtain the model-specific instructions before use. If you later order a different configuration, ask for its information separately.

A demonstration unit helps your team plan handling and presentation. Ask the importer or local specialist to check applicable use requirements for the exact model and market.
