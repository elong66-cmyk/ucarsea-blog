Include the required steering position when asking a supplier to find a car. Specify left-hand drive (LHD) or right-hand drive (RHD), then say whether you can consider the other version. Naming the steering wheel position directly avoids confusion with the side of the road used in the destination.

## Tie the check to the proposed vehicle

Ask for the exact model, trim and listing reference. A clear dashboard photo can show the steering wheel position on the photographed vehicle. Ask the supplier to confirm that the photo belongs to the unit they are proposing, especially if several versions share a model name. If a listing is used only as an example, leave the final unit’s specification open until it is identified.

## Keep registration as a separate check

Give the seller the destination country and explain the intended use. Confirm the applicable import and registration requirements separately for that vehicle before committing to an order. A photo and a steering-position label describe the vehicle. Registration depends on the rules and documentation that apply to the proposed transaction.

## Put it in the first request

“Please look for [make, model and trim], [quantity], for [destination]. The steering wheel must be on the [left/right]. For each proposed unit, share the listing reference and a matching dashboard photo. We will confirm the destination requirements before committing.”
