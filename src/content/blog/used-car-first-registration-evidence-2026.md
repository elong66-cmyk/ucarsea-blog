A listing can show a model year and a different year for first registration. Give each field its own line in your shortlist. Ask the seller to confirm the dates for the exact vehicle you are considering and to say which source supports each one.

## Ask for the source behind each date

Record the vehicle reference, listed model year, reported build date and first registration date. Ask for the month as well as the year when the seller has it. A model brochure can help identify a trim. For the selected car’s registration date, ask for a dated record that can be matched to that car. Share a redacted copy with colleagues when personal details are unnecessary for their review.

## Keep a mismatch visible to the buyer

If a listing and a document give different dates, record both and ask the seller to explain the difference. Before confirming an order, give the relevant dates and destination to the person checking import eligibility. Ask which date they need and what document they require for that review. Record the answer beside the exact vehicle reference and update the shortlist when verified evidence arrives.
