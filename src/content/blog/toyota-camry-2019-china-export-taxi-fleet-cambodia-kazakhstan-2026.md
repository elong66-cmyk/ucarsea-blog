## The car that never needs explaining

Ask a taxi operator in Phnom Penh, a ride-hailing fleet owner in Almaty or a family buyer in Lagos what sedan they want and the answer is the same: a Camry. Not because it is exciting, but because every mechanic in those cities has worked on one, every parts shop stocks it, and the buyer already knows the resale price before we quote.

That is also why the price gap on a Camry is narrow. This unit sits about 7% under our benchmark. We show that number rather than hide it, because a dealer who only sees "cheap" listings has no way to judge which ones are real.

## What 100,000 km means on this car

Six figures on the odometer scares buyers who think in European or Japanese terms. On a Camry 2.0 with the eight-generation body it means something different:

**The 2.0 engine and CVT pairing is the fleet spec.** This is the powertrain Toyota sold into taxi fleets across Asia. Units in ride-hailing service routinely pass 300,000 km on the original engine and gearbox with nothing more than fluid changes.

**Highway kilometres wear less than city kilometres.** A 2019 car at 100,000 km has averaged about 14,000 km a year, which in China usually means commuting and intercity driving rather than stop-start taxi work. The service record tells you which it was.

**The price already reflects the mileage.** A 40,000 km unit of the same year would list closer to $18,000. The fleet buyer who runs the car to 300,000 km is paying $3,500 less for the same remaining life.

## Where it makes money

**Cambodia.** Right-hand-drive imports are banned, so a left-hand-drive Camry from China lands legally where a Japanese-market unit cannot. Taxi and rental operators in Phnom Penh and Siem Reap are the volume buyers.

**Kazakhstan.** The Camry is the default executive and fleet sedan in Almaty and Astana. A 2019 unit clears the age rules, and petrol keeps it out of the EV duty discussion entirely.

**West Africa.** Nigeria and Ghana buyers know the Camry from the American import route. A China-sourced unit is typically cheaper landed than a US salvage-title car and comes with a clean history.

## Three checks before a deposit

1. **Service stamps against the odometer.** 100,000 km is fine if the oil changes line up with it. Ask for the record, not just the number.
2. **CVT fluid condition.** The one component that separates a taxi-grade Camry from a tired one. Ask for a fluid photo or a diagnostic readout.
3. **Underbody and tyre wear.** Uneven tyre wear on a Camry usually means a curb strike. Underbody photos are in the inspection set.

## Bottom line

A Camry is not where a dealer finds a 30% margin. It is where a dealer finds a car that sells the week it lands, to a buyer who already trusts it. At $14,511 with a full record, this one is priced to move.
