## The SKU

**Toyota Levin 2024, 185T Luxury** — 35,000 km, one owner, LHD, 1.2 turbo petrol, full photo set on file.

- **EXW price**: $12,381
- **Market benchmark**: $26,345 (10 comparable listings)
- **Spread**: 53% below benchmark

Ten comparables is our minimum sample, so treat the benchmark as a floor for the discussion rather than a headline. Even at half that spread the unit prices well under a comparable Corolla landed from Japan or the Gulf.

## What the Levin actually is

The Levin is built by GAC Toyota for the Chinese market. It shares the Corolla's platform, the 1.2T engine family and most of the mechanical parts bin. The differences are in front and rear styling and trim naming. A mechanic who services Corollas will not find anything unfamiliar under the bonnet.

Two things it is **not**:

- It is not a Japanese-market Corolla. Badging, infotainment language and some trim parts are China-spec. Say so in your own listing.
- It is not a right-hand-drive car. For Kenya, Uganda and Tanzania this matters. For Nigeria and Ghana it does not.

## Why the price gap exists

China's used market discounts Toyota sedans far more than African markets do. Supply is deep, buyers there are moving to electric, and a 2024 Levin with 35,000 km is simply not a scarce object in Guangzhou. In Lagos it is.

That gap is the margin. It is not a defect and it is not a distressed unit. The inspection report on this car shows no structural repair and consistent paint thickness across all panels.

## Two checks before you order

1. **Drive side and market**: confirm your destination is LHD. We will not ship a Levin to an RHD market and call it a Corolla.
2. **Parts channel**: ask your local Toyota parts supplier to confirm Corolla 1.2T filters, pads and belts cross-reference. In every case we have checked they do, but the confirmation should be yours.
