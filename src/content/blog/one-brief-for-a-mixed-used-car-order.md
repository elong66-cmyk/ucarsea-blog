A dealer may want two compact sedans and one family SUV for the same destination. Put them in one enquiry with separate rows. A single line that says “three cars under budget” leaves the supplier guessing which requirement belongs to which vehicle.

### Give the whole request a common destination and scope

Write down the destination country and city or port once. State your quotation basis and currency. If there is a total spending limit, specify what is included in that limit. A vehicle-only cap and a cap that includes some transport costs will produce different shortlists.

### Specify one row for each group of vehicles

| Group | Quantity | Required | Flexible |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Compact sedan | 2 | Left-hand drive and automatic | Colour and choice between the listed models |
| Family SUV | 1 | Left-hand drive and the agreed seating capacity | Colour and trim features that are not essential |

This example is a request format. Add the year range, acceptable condition and price cap for each row where they affect your decision. If one row cannot be matched, say whether the other rows can be reviewed separately or whether you need a new proposal for the complete order.

### Read back the shortlist before deciding

Ask for vehicle-specific photos or references, the current price basis and outstanding condition checks for each option. Any transport or consolidation estimate should identify the vehicles it covers. Confirm availability and the quote scope for the units you select before arranging payment.

Use the [vehicle sourcing form](https://ucarsea.com/sourcing/) and place your separate rows in the request notes. The form collects your requirements and does not reserve inventory.
