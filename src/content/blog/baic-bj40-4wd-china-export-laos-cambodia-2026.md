- The **BAIC BJ40** is a body-on-frame, low-range 4WD SUV — a genuine off-roader, not a crossover styled to look rugged.
- A 2021 **Blade Hero Xiake Edition** unit with **40,000 km clears EXW at $16,136** against a China domestic benchmark of $25,746 (n=72) — a 37.3% discount, comfortably inside our credible-comparison band.
- This SKU sells to buyers who actually need low-range 4WD: rural transport operators, hill-terrain fleets, and dealers in markets like Laos and Cambodia where paved-road assumptions don't hold outside the main cities.
- 72 comparable listings back the benchmark — one of the larger sample sizes we quote against, which makes the spread easier to trust than most.

## Why this SKU, and who it is not for

Most "SUV" listings in cross-border used-export pipelines are unibody crossovers with AWD badging and no real off-road capability. The BJ40 is different: body-on-frame construction, a low-range transfer case, and a chassis architecture built for the same duty cycle as a Jeep Wrangler or Suzuki Jimny, not a RAV4. That distinction matters more in landed markets than it does in the listing photos.

Sell it to:
- Rural transport operators and cooperatives running routes where seasonal flooding or unpaved terrain rules out a standard sedan or crossover.
- Hill-province dealers in Laos and northern Cambodia, where genuine 4WD capability is a selling point buyers actively ask for, not a marketing checkbox.
- Fleet buyers needing a rugged utility vehicle for agriculture, logging-adjacent transport, or field operations rather than daily city commuting.

Do not sell it to:
- Urban buyers who just want SUV styling — a crossover will ride better and cost less to run in city conditions.
- Markets with strict emissions or age-based import caps on older 4WD platforms — verify local rules before quoting a 2021 unit.
- Buyers expecting soft, car-like ride quality — body-on-frame 4WD platforms trade comfort for capability, and that trade-off should be set clearly before the sale, not discovered after delivery.

## Why the discount is this wide

China's domestic used-SUV market treats the BJ40 as a niche enthusiast/utility vehicle rather than a mainstream family SUV, which keeps resale demand — and therefore domestic pricing — softer than the vehicle's actual capability would suggest in an export market that values 4WD more highly. A 72-listing sample backing the $25,746 median gives us confidence this is a genuine market gap rather than a thin, unreliable comparison — our internal gate treats anything under 10 samples as unreliable, and 72 is one of the stronger samples we've quoted against this month.
