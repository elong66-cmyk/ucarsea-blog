## Why n=43 matters here

We do not release a comparison estimate off a handful of listings. Our internal rule: no release below 10 comparables, and anything with a spread above 60% or below 15% gets flagged as suspect — usually a data error, a mismatched trim, or a salvage unit hiding in the sample.

A 29.7% spread on **43 comparables** sits comfortably inside the defensible range. This is a real number, not a marketing headline.

## Landed cost by market

The VS5's compact footprint and 1.4T fuel economy make it a strong fit for markets where daily-driver economics — not badge status — drive the buy decision:

- **Cambodia**: right-hand-drive conversion not required (LHD market); duty structure favors compact-displacement imports over larger SUVs.
- **Laos**: land-border logistics from Yunnan/Guangxi keep freight cost low relative to sea-freight markets — this is one of the fastest-clearing corridors for compact Chinese-market SUVs.
- **Vietnam**: higher duty tier on ICE imports, but the EXW-to-benchmark spread still leaves room after landed cost — worth a quote before assuming margin is gone.
- **Central Asia (Kazakhstan/Uzbekistan/Tajikistan)**: growing appetite for compact SUVs over sedans; VS5's ground clearance and turbo torque outperform naturally-aspirated competitors at this price point.

Always confirm current duty tables per destination before final quoting — see our [tariff reference tables](https://ucarsea.com/wholesale-price-list/) for current rates by country.

- **Buyers expecting a mid-size or full-size SUV** — this is a compact-class vehicle; set size expectations up front.
- **High-duty ICE markets without a clear resale channel** — confirm landed cost before committing to volume.
