## A premium badge, an unpriced segment

Most ASEAN premium-EV shelf space is Tesla, or a handful of Chinese luxury EVs still building brand recognition. BMW's iX3 sits in neither camp — established badge, EV drivetrain, and almost no used-market presence in the region yet. That gap is the opportunity: a 2024 iX3 (imported spec), 40,000 km, priced at **$26,737 EXW** against a **$44,684** benchmark built from 30 comparable listings. A 40% spread on a car your customers already trust by name.

## Why the spread is this wide

BMW's China-market iX3 production feeds a domestic market large enough to generate real turnover — meaning real comparable listings, not a handful of grey-import outliers. That volume is what makes a credible 30-listing benchmark possible in the first place, and it's what keeps the export price honest rather than opportunistic.

The badge premium that inflates a new iX3's price tag in Gulf and Southeast Asian showrooms doesn't carry over proportionally to the used China-export price — the car depreciates against China-market comparables, not against the price a first-market buyer paid.

## What changes with an EV compared to a petrol BMW import

**Battery health replaces engine compression as the inspection priority.** Ask for the onboard battery health reading before anything else — this is the number that determines resale value three years out, not mileage alone.

**Charging infrastructure readiness matters more than fuel-station density.** Confirm your market has functioning DC fast-charging before marketing this as a daily driver rather than a second car — this is a customer-education point, not a sourcing one, but it affects how fast the unit turns.

**Duty treatment varies more than for petrol imports.** Several markets in this region (Cambodia, parts of Central Asia) run EV import duty at zero or near-zero specifically to encourage EV adoption — check your local schedule before benchmarking margin against a petrol BMW import, since the duty gap alone can be a five-figure swing.

## Positioning it on the lot

This is not a volume SKU. It is a one-or-two-unit premium-badge play for a dealer who wants an EV on the lot that customers recognize without having to explain the brand first. Price it against the badge, not against the segment average.
