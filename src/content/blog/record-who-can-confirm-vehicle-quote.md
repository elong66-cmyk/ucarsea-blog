The person who finds a vehicle, the person who checks the destination costs and the person who approves the purchase may be three different people. Record who can confirm each part of the quote so your supplier knows which message is a decision and who can approve a change.

### Name the roles in the initial enquiry

Give one business contact for the request. Inside your team, identify who approves the vehicle choice, who checks the destination costs and who can authorise a purchase decision. One person can hold several roles. Share only the business contact details needed for the enquiry.

### Keep a short decision record

For every proposal you discuss, keep the date, the vehicle reference, the price basis, the currency and the items still open. Attach the latest quotation or give it a version number. If your local logistics contact thinks a charge has changed, record what changed and ask for a revised quotation before the buyer approves it.

### Confirm in one message

When your team is ready to proceed, have the authorised person confirm which vehicle and quote version they are accepting. For a change of vehicle, destination or payment details, ask for fresh written confirmation through your established business contact before proceeding. A forwarded screenshot is useful context, but it may omit scope or show a previous version.

If you are still collecting the details, start with the [vehicle sourcing form](https://ucarsea.com/sourcing/) and indicate which points your team still needs to confirm.
