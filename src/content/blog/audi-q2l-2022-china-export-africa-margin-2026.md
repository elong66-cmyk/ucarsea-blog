## Where it makes money

**West Africa (Ghana, Nigeria).** Premium compacts clear at a meaningful multiple of landed cost when the buyer is upgrading from a mainstream sedan, not cross-shopping against other SUVs. Confirm your import age window before you commit — some West African ports treat 2022 differently than 2021.

**East Africa (Kenya, Ethiopia).** A 2022 Audi at this landed cost undercuts nearly every used-premium listing already on the ground, because most premium-badge stock arrives from Gulf re-export at Gulf pricing, not China-direct pricing.

**Middle East wholesale.** Dealers who buy in bulk for their own retail lots use spreads like this to hit a target margin band without discounting the badge.
