## The comparison dealers keep skipping

Most ASEAN Tesla resupply still runs through Japan auction houses, because that is the route everyone learned first. It is also the more expensive route. A 2022 Model Y RWD (facelift), 60,000 km, priced at **$26,433 EXW** out of China against a Japan-market benchmark of **$36,771** built from 22 comparable listings — a $10,338 gap on the same spec.

That gap exists because China is Tesla's largest production base, not a secondary market re-exporting someone else's surplus. Shanghai Gigafactory output means more units changing hands domestically, more comparable listings to benchmark against, and less markup from a re-export middleman.

## Why the China-market unit isn't a compromise

**Same factory, same battery.** Model Y built for the China market and Model Y built for Japan's domestic market both come out of Giga Shanghai for most trims. There is no hidden regional downgrade — the difference is which market absorbed the car first, not how it was built.

**Battery health is verifiable, not assumed.** Tesla's onboard diagnostics report battery degradation directly. Ask for the in-app battery health screen or a service-center printout before committing — this is a five-minute check that removes the single biggest resale-value question on any used EV.

**Software carries over.** Over-the-air updates are tied to the vehicle, not the market it was delivered in. A China-market unit continues receiving the same update stream after export.

## What to verify before ordering

1. **Accident history and paint meter reading** — this unit shows zero accidents and original paint; confirm both independently, not just from the listing.
2. **Battery health percentage** — pull it from the car, not the seller's description.
3. **Charging standard compatibility** — confirm your market's connector type against the unit's onboard charger before shipping, not after arrival.
4. **Drivetrain match to your climate** — RWD units are fine for most ASEAN markets; confirm before assuming AWD is required.

## The margin math

At $26,433 EXW landed against a $36,771 benchmark, the spread survives freight, duty and a dealer margin in most ASEAN import regimes — check your local EV duty schedule first, since several markets (Cambodia, parts of Central Asia) run EV import duty at zero or near-zero, which widens this spread further rather than eating into it.
