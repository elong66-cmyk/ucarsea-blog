A dealer's receiving team may have the stock list and arrival date but none of the notes the buyer gathered before shipment. A short exception list gives the team the agreed baseline for the first inspection at arrival.

### Keep the list tied to the selected car

Use one row per vehicle and write down its agreed reference, the date of the latest condition evidence and the exception. Examples include a reported repaint, an accessory that is missing or a function the seller could not demonstrate. Separate the seller's report, an independent finding and a check that is still outstanding. Point to the relevant photo or document instead of copying a long chat.

### Record what changed

At receipt, record who checked the car and when. Photograph the condition or item if it differs from the documented baseline. Record whether the issue was expected, is newly observed or needs further inspection. Keep timing, responsibility and any resolution request in a separate note according to the order terms.

Before arranging your next order, ask who on your team will receive the vehicles and what evidence that person needs.
