## Why a plug-in, not a full EV, is the realistic Iraq play

Ethiopia can ban petrol imports outright because it is building charging infrastructure around a national EV mandate. Iraq and Kurdistan are not doing that — public charging is sparse outside Erbil and Baghdad's newer districts, and grid reliability is its own separate problem. A full battery EV is a hard sell to a dealer who has to explain to a buyer where they charge it.

A plug-in hybrid sidesteps the question. The BYD Song Pro DM-i runs a 75km all-electric range for daily driving and a petrol engine for everything past that — the buyer never has to find a charger to keep driving. This is the version of "EV-adjacent" that actually works in a market with unreliable charging, not the marketing version.

## Why this trim specifically

**75km EV range covers most Erbil or Baghdad commutes fully electric**, with the petrol engine only engaging on longer runs — lower real-world fuel cost than a comparable petrol-only compact SUV, without asking the buyer to solve charging.

**DM-i is BYD's volume platform, not a low-run niche trim.** n=108 on the benchmark reflects that — parts availability and comparable resale data exist because enough units are already in circulation, unlike a boutique EV model where the next buyer has no reference price.

**Near-zero mileage reduces the inspection burden.** At 2,000 km this is functionally a new car with a used-car price tag — battery degradation and mechanical wear are non-issues at this point in the car's life.

## What to verify before ordering

1. **Hybrid battery health check** — even at 2,000 km, request the diagnostic readout; this is the one component that's expensive to replace and cheap to verify upfront.
2. **Charging cable and connector standard** — confirm the included Type 2 or GB/T cable matches what's actually installable in your market before committing to shipping.
3. **Regional parts network** — check whether your local BYD service presence (if any) covers DM-i platform components before promising after-sales support to your buyer.
4. **Fuel-grade requirement** — confirm the petrol engine's octane requirement against your local pump-grade fuel; this is a common mismatch importers skip checking.

## The margin math

At $13,484 EXW against a $17,501 benchmark, the spread holds up after freight and duty in most cases — Kurdistan's semi-autonomous import handling and Iraq's federal import lanes have different duty treatment, so confirm your specific entry point's PHEV duty schedule before quoting a landed price to your buyer.
