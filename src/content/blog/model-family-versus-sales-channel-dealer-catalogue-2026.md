A useful vehicle catalogue starts with the buyer's task. An importer building a dealer range, a shop considering a display model and an individual comparing one vehicle may need different information, even when they are looking at the same photograph.

## Keep three questions separate

First, identify the product family and the exact model reference. Two-wheel motorcycles, three-wheel vehicles and other mobility products should not be grouped together simply because they are electric. Keep the relevant seating, cargo arrangement and configuration with each reference.

Second, record the buyer's role. Is the recipient evaluating a wholesale range, planning a fleet, selecting a showroom sample or asking about an individual purchase? That role helps determine which information belongs in the first comparison. It does not prove that the recipient has a purchase requirement or has agreed to become a distributor.

Third, identify the commercial supply format. A catalogue may describe a sample option, a trade quotation or a container-based arrangement. Keep those distinctions visible. A dealer product page should not be presented as a confirmed single-unit retail offer when the underlying supply arrangement has not established one.

## Build a short comparison

Use one line per model: reference, product family, intended buyer role, configuration included in the comparison and the supply format currently described. Mark missing information rather than copying an assumption from a similar-looking vehicle.

Keep destination-specific documents, delivery arrangements and the final quotation with the concrete order discussion. An attractive image or a broad category name does not replace that work. Avoid presenting an unverified performance, registration or medical-use statement as a product benefit.

## Send a focused brief

Share the model references, buyer type, destination and proposed quantity. UCarsea can use that brief to structure the sourcing discussion and narrow the catalogue. Configuration, documentation and commercial terms are confirmed for the actual order.
