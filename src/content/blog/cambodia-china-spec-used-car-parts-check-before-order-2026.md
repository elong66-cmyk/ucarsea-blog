An internationally familiar badge does not prove that every China-market trim uses the same parts as the cars already serviced by your workshop. Before ordering stock for Cambodia, treat local repairability as a sourcing question, not an assumption.

## Identify the actual unit

Ask for the vehicle identity, model year, trim, engine or motor specification, gearbox type and fuel system. Keep the evidence linked to the same unit. A parts enquiry based only on “Civic” or “Corolla” can refer to a different generation, powertrain or market specification.

Use the identity details privately with the seller and workshop. Do not post customer or payment documents in public groups to obtain a parts answer.

## Have a local workshop check the ordinary items first

Start with service filters, brakes, tyres, cooling components and common electrical parts. Ask your local supplier for the exact compatible part, sourcing route, current availability and indicative lead time. “We can order it” is not the same as “we stock it,” and neither guarantees the future price.

For a hybrid or battery-electric vehicle, add diagnostics compatibility, charging hardware, battery-service capability and the evidence needed to assess the specific battery. A photograph of a charging socket does not establish local charging compatibility or battery health.

## Use a short parts-readiness worksheet

| Item | Question to resolve | Record |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicle identity | Which exact unit and trim? | Seller evidence reference |
| Routine service | Which part numbers are compatible? | Workshop confirmation |
| Repair supply | Stocked locally or ordered abroad? | Supplier and dated estimate |
| Diagnostics | Can the workshop read relevant systems? | Tool and technician confirmation |
| After-sales | Who pays for which issue? | Written commercial terms |

Leave unanswered fields open. A short unresolved list is a reason to investigate, not a reason to invent a warranty promise.

## Keep repairability and import eligibility separate

A workshop's ability to repair a car does not establish its import eligibility, customs treatment or registration status. Those need current checks for the exact specification and intended transaction. This guide does not assert a Cambodian tariff, import ban, age limit or exemption.

## Use the answers to shape a smaller shortlist

If the first model needs uncertain overseas parts, compare an alternative that your intended workshop can support. Consider the inspection results, supply costs and buyer's intended use alongside the opening vehicle price. Do not presume that a familiar logo automatically solves the whole after-sales question.
