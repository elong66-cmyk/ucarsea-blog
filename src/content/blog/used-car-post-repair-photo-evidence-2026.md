When a used-car listing mentions repainting or a replaced part, ask which photos show the vehicle as it stands now. Tie the answer to the exact listing or vehicle reference so your team can separate the seller’s current photos from earlier stock images.

## Request a dated set for the affected area

Ask the seller to identify the affected area in their own words. Request a wide shot for orientation, a closer photo of each named area and a dated note showing when the photos were taken. If a new image replaces one in the shared gallery, keep the date and the reason for the replacement on your order checklist.

## Keep the repair disclosure separate from the photo review

Photos help your team identify the panel and see visible details. Record the repair as seller-reported unless you have separate evidence. If the scope or timing is unclear, put that question on the independent inspection checklist and ask what the inspection can verify before your team confirms the order.
