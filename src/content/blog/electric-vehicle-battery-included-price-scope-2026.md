Two prices for the same model name may describe different packages. Before comparing them, identify whether each includes the vehicle, a specified battery and a specified charger. A separate battery line is useful information and should not be overlooked or repeatedly requested.

## Read the existing quotation first

Start with the supplier's model and configuration reference. Copy the vehicle price, battery option and charger scope into separate lines, preserving the currency and quotation date. If the battery already has a separate stated price, use that line in the comparison rather than asking the supplier for the same price again.

Record “not stated” when an item is absent. Do not turn a product photograph into evidence that the pictured battery or charger is included in the quoted amount. Similarly, do not assume that an alternative battery price applies to every version sharing a body design.

## Keep technical fit separate from price arithmetic

Adding a vehicle-only price and a battery price can describe a proposed package only when the quotation links those options. Electrical compatibility, mounting, connectors and the charging setup still belong to the exact configuration review. This checklist is not an instruction to combine or modify battery systems.

For a destination discussion, distinguish the proposed charger from the local supply requirements. Do not promise that an unspecified charger will work in every country. Battery transport documents also concern the actual battery and shipment, rather than a general brand name.

## Write the comparison in one place

Use columns for vehicle reference, battery reference, charger, included accessories and excluded costs. Leave unresolved items visible. A reference package total is not a final destination-inclusive quotation.
