Two offers for the same used-car model can include different sets of keys and accessories. One seller may have two working keys. Another may show a second key in the listing without confirming that it starts the selected vehicle. Record what will be handed over before you compare the offers.

### Ask for the delivered items

Start with the exact vehicle reference and ask how many keys are included. Ask which buttons the seller can demonstrate and whether each key can start the car. If the seller cannot test a key, record the missing check. Keep loose accessories on the same list, such as an owner's manual, a removable luggage cover or a supplied charging cable if the vehicle uses one. Ask for dated photos of the actual items.

### Turn any missing item into a decision

Ask your local service contact whether an exact replacement can be sourced and what information is needed to check compatibility. Record the estimate and decide whether to accept the vehicle as offered or request a change before the order. At handover, match the items to the vehicle reference and record discrepancies separately.

If you send a sourcing brief, include the vehicle reference and the items you require at handover.
