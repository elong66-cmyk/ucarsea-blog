**Before discussing a replacement part, identify the supplied vehicle configuration and the component being requested.** A familiar model name or a similar-looking photograph is a starting point for an enquiry, rather than confirmation that a particular item is suitable.

For example, Freego's public [LCD display listing](https://freegobikes.com/products/lcd-displayer-dk200) presents separate model selections. That is evidence of how that catalogue organises an item, not proof that a selected display will fit a vehicle supplied under a different configuration. The listing was reviewed on 1 November 2026; its retail price, availability and regional terms are not UCarsea quotations or commitments.

## Prepare a specific enquiry

Record the vehicle model and supplied configuration alongside the component name. If a part number is available, preserve it exactly. Include clear photographs of the original component and its label through an appropriate business channel, without publishing unrelated customer information.

Ask the responsible supplier to confirm which item is proposed and what information supports the match. A photograph alone leaves unanswered questions. Do not approve a substitution merely because the exterior looks similar.

## Separate the decisions

| Decision | Evidence to request |
|---|---|
| Component identification | Original label or part number and the proposed replacement reference |
| Configuration match | Supplier confirmation tied to the supplied vehicle details |
| Availability | A current confirmation for that particular item |
| Cost and delivery | Quantity, currency, included costs and a separately confirmed delivery scope |
| Responsibilities | The parties' agreed handling of replacement, installation and any applicable support terms |

These are procurement questions. They are not installation instructions or a diagnosis. Any installation or safety assessment should be handled by the appropriately qualified responsible party using the applicable manufacturer information.

Keep the enquiry, proposed replacement, compatibility confirmation and accepted order as distinct records. If an essential detail remains unknown, leave the request open rather than treating the existence of a catalogue page as completion.

For an adult electric-motorcycle sourcing discussion, share the model, intended country, quantity and the parts-support questions your team needs to resolve through [UCarsea Moto](https://moto.ucarsea.com/). A sourcing enquiry does not itself confirm stock, compatibility, delivery time or warranty coverage.
